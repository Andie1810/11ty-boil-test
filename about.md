## About

This blog is a record of my journey beyond the mapped edges of human space — a personal archive of observations, encounters, failures, and moments of wonder that would otherwise vanish into the vacuum. Out here, distance reshapes everything: time stretches, memories blur, and even the smallest discoveries feel monumental.

I write to remember what it means to be human when there is no one else around to reflect it back to me.

Some entries are technical — notes on anomalies, planetary surveys, or navigation challenges. Others are quieter: thoughts that surface during long transits between stars, reflections on solitude, or the strange beauty of places untouched by life.

If these transmissions ever reach another person, then this mission was never truly lonely.
